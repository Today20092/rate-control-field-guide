# Surface brief: home (the whole guide)

Scope: the single long page `src/pages/index.astro`. Mode: Read. Audience and chapters: see PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Animators named the idea first: key drawings, breakdowns and in-betweens are I, P and B frames. The page refuses the category default (white cards on a grey grid, a sticky blue table of contents) and is set as a working animation sheet.

OWN-WORLD: Cool animation-bond paper (#f4f6f4) with a peg-hole strip heading the page and each chapter opener; graphite ink (#1d2326) for text; red key pencil (#d8432b) only for I-frames/keys and the current position; non-photo blue (#6fa8dc) only for motion vectors, predictions and labels; peg tan (#e9e2c8) for the peg strip. Diagrams are flat planes lit from one side, no gradients or glow. Bricolage Grotesque display, Literata reading face, JetBrains Mono for frame numbers and labels.

STORY: The reader learns one picture, then many, then that video stores changes, not pictures; then codecs, bitrate, trade-offs and the settings that control them. They leave able to explain bitrate and I/P/B.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Peg strip on top; a left timing-chart rail of chapter ticks (current tick red and thick); title "Video doesn't store pictures. It stores changes." at ~88px with "changes" in red; a short lede; a 3D frame stack below with keys in red, in-betweens dashed; right margin exposure sheet whose bar lengths equal bits per frame.

FORM: The Animator's Exposure Sheet, candidate 4 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:48a369323ef9e3bc. Raises: flat planes (Precisionist), exact lengths (Labanotation), visible structure in the rail (Coptic), four distinct buffer states (Tensegrity).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
The 3D frame stack fanning apart to show that only the red keys are full pictures and the rest are blue motion arrows plus small corrections.
